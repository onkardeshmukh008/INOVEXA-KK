SkillTrace.Ai — Upskill your skills with AI
SkillTrace.Ai is an AI-powered platform that helps students understand their skills, prepare for job opportunities, and improve their career readiness.

The platform helps users compare their skills with job requirements, identify areas for improvement, and track their progress through assessments and reminders.

🚀 Features

- AI-Powered Skill Assessment: Assess your current skills and understand how prepared you are for a job.
- Career Readiness Insights: Identify your strengths and the areas where you need to improve.
- In-App Notifications: Receive reminders and updates directly within the application.
- Gmail Notifications: Get task reminders and updates through Gmail to help you stay organized.
- Task Reminders: Remember important tasks and stay focused on your career goals.
- Multiple Assessments: Explore assessments that help you understand your knowledge and improve your preparation.
- Skill Gap Identification: Understand which skills you may need to develop based on your target job requirements.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to make career preparation easier, more organized, and more accessible for students. SkillTrace.ai helps users understand where they stand today and what they can do to become better prepared for their future careers.

👥 Who Can Use SkillTrace.ai?

- Students preparing for internships and placements
- Beginners who want to understand their current skills
- Job seekers looking to improve their career readiness
- Learners who want to track tasks and improve their knowledge

💡 Why SkillTrace.Ai?

Preparing for a job can feel confusing when you do not know which skills to focus on. SkillTrace.ai brings assessments, skill insights, and helpful reminders together in one place so users can plan their learning and work toward their career goals.

🛠️ Technology

SkillTrace.ai uses AI and web technologies to support skill assessments, career readiness insights, and notifications. The exact technologies and configuration depend on the current project implementation.

⚙️ Getting Started

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder in your code editor.
3. Install the project dependencies using the package manager specified by the project.
4. Configure the required environment variables in your local environment.
5. Start the development server using the command specified in "package.json".

Keep API keys and other secret values private. Do not commit them to GitHub.
